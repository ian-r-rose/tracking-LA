Agenda items from meetings November 13 – 19, 2026, and decisions recorded from Council journals, committee records and Board of Public Works minutes. 14 agenda items reviewed; 3 listed below, plus decisions on earlier items.

## Planning and Land Use Management Committee

The Council committee that reviews zoning, planning and appeal items before they go to the full Council.

- **Oct 27** · Mid-Wilshire — Appeals of the seven-story, 161,645 sq ft self-storage building at 4802–4828 W San Vicente Blvd: **denied, 13-0-2**, upholding the City Planning Commission's approval. The related zone and height district change (floor area ratio from 1.5:1 to 6:1) was also approved. Council adopted both Nov 6; action final on the appeals. [Council File 26-1289](https://lms.lacity.gov/files/26-1289-S1)
- **Oct 27** · Mount Washington — Appeal of the approval of two SB 9 homes at 1236 N Cliff Dr: **denied, 13-0-2**; Council action final. [Council File 26-1311](https://lms.lacity.gov/files/26-1311)

[Council File 13-0078](https://lms.lacity.gov/files/13-0078-S2)
- **Oct 13** · Downtown — Vesting zone change for the two-tower project at 2117–2147 E Violet St and 2118–2142 E 7th Pl (474 live/work units, 77 affordable): **approved 13-0-2**; Council adopted Oct 27, action final. [Council File 21-0784](https://lms.lacity.gov/files/21-0784-S1)
- **Oct 13** · Citywide — Ordinance carrying out state SB 79 (homes near transit) through low-rise zoning rules and the Mixed Income Incentive Program: **approved 13-0-2**; Council adopted Oct 27, action final. [Council File 25-1083](https://lms.lacity.gov/files/25-1083-S3)
