## City Planning Commission

Decides major projects, citywide plans and code changes, and appeals.

Part of the Missing Middle LA program. - **Nov 12** · upcoming · citywide — The Commission would recommend updated small lot subdivision rules and a new Starter Home ordinance with an optional bonus program, to allow more smaller-scale homes. [Agenda](https://planning.lacity.gov/dcpapi2/meetings/document/81670) · [Staff report](https://planning.lacity.gov/plndoc/Staff_Reports/2026/11-12-2026/CPC_2025_4421_CA__CPC_2025_4430_CA__CPC_2026_4383_CA.pdf)
- **Nov 12** · upcoming · citywide — The Commission would recommend requiring lot amenity space in developments of fewer than six units and extending outdoor amenity standards to single-family zones. [Agenda](https://planning.lacity.gov/dcpapi2/meetings/document/81670) · [Staff report](https://planning.lacity.gov/plndoc/Staff_Reports/2026/11-12-2026/CPC_2025_4421_CA__CPC_2025_4430_CA__CPC_2026_4383_CA.pdf)
