# Commissions digest — September 30, 2026

This first digest covers Planning commission agendas from July through the next two weeks. Most of these meetings already happened, and decisions aren't tracked yet. The one coming up is the **LA River frontage at the Atwater Village water reclamation plant, decided Oct 8**.

## Coming up

- **City Planning Commission, Oct 8** · Atwater Village — LA Sanitation's expansion of the LA–Glendale Water Reclamation Plant (4590 W Colorado Blvd): new treatment tanks, a staff building, and a water purification demonstration facility. Along the river it would add about 18,000 sq ft of **publicly accessible open space** with paths, seating and shade trees. It would also add an **8-ft fence with a gate onto the river bike path**, above the 6-ft limit in the River Implementation Overlay, which needs Commission approval. The public hearing closed in July; the Commission votes Oct 8. [Agenda](https://planning.lacity.gov/dcpapi2/meetings/document/81511) · [Staff report](https://planning.lacity.gov/plndoc/Staff_Reports/2026/10-08-2026/CPC_2025_5724.pdf)
