# Commissions digest — September 30, 2026

Agenda items from meetings July 7 – October 8, 2026. 50 items reviewed; 16 listed below.

## City Planning Commission

Decides major land-use cases citywide and recommends zoning and plan changes to the City Council.

- **Jul 9** · Highland Park — A 7-unit density-bonus building (2 affordable units) behind a 1907 duplex at 217 N Avenue 55, inside the Highland Park–Garvanza Historic Preservation Overlay Zone. A test case for infill in a historic district. [Agenda](https://planning.lacity.gov/dcpapi2/meetings/document/81051) · [Staff report](https://planning.lacity.gov/plndoc/Staff_Reports/2026/06-30-2026/CPC_2023_3247.pdf)
- **Oct 8** · upcoming · Atwater Village — LA Sanitation's expansion of the LA–Glendale Water Reclamation Plant (4590 W Colorado Blvd): new treatment tanks, a staff building, and a water purification demonstration facility. Along the LA River it would add about 18,000 sq ft of **publicly accessible open space** with paths, seating and shade trees. It would also add an **8-ft fence with a gate onto the river bike path**, above the 6-ft limit in the River Implementation Overlay. The public hearing closed in July; this meeting is the vote. [Agenda](https://planning.lacity.gov/dcpapi2/meetings/document/81511) · [Staff report](https://planning.lacity.gov/plndoc/Staff_Reports/2026/10-08-2026/CPC_2025_5724.pdf)
